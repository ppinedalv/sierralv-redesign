Frequently asked questions.

Straight answers about how we manage your property — and what it costs.

Costs & getting started

Fees

What does SierraLV charge?

A 9% monthly management fee on rent collected, a one-time $175 set-up fee, a $350 leasing fee when a new tenant is placed, and a $100 annual inspection fee. Rekeys (up to $120) are paid by the tenant. There are no upfront costs, no charges while your property is vacant, and no renewal fee. See the full fee table.

Do I pay anything while my home is vacant?

No. We don't charge management fees on vacant properties — the 9% fee applies only to rent that's collected.

How long will it take my property to rent?

It depends mostly on price. We start with a current market comparison of similar homes and amenities that leased in your neighborhood over the past six months. Listing too high without evidence that the home is worth more almost guarantees extra vacancy.

Priced fairly for its location and features, a home can usually expect to rent within 30 days or less.

Is a home warranty worth the cost?

For homes older than about 10 years, we've found it usually is. Plumbing, fixtures, electrical, garage doors, water heaters and HVAC systems tend to show wear after that point, and having those repairs covered saves money over the long run.

Tenants & leases

Residents

Why does a good tenant relationship matter to me as an owner?

Happy renters make happy property owners. Residents who are treated well tend to take better care of the home, pay on time, follow the lease and take responsibility when issues arise. Most importantly, they stay longer — which means less turnover and vacancy for you.

Are tenants required to carry renter's insurance?

Absolutely. It's part of every lease, and coverage must begin on the first day of occupancy. If a resident ever lets their policy lapse, our lease allows us to automatically enroll them in a landlord liability policy that they pay for monthly, which covers accidental tenant damage.

How do you decide whether a tenant can renew?

Renewal isn't automatic. Every resident must pass an annual inspection, and we also weigh on-time payment history, any HOA violations, and the volume of maintenance and repairs during the lease. The goal is to find — and keep — only the best tenants for your property.

What happens if a tenant pays rent late?

We take on-time rent seriously. Every resident receives clear expectations about due dates and late rent up front, and we enforce them consistently — which protects your cash flow and establishes clear grounds for action if it's ever needed.

Maintenance & money

Repairs & reporting

Can SierraLV handle major renovations or repairs?

Yes. Our property managers have a long track record helping owners with large repairs and remodels, and we work with contractors and vendors we've used many times with great results. Before a project begins, we evaluate what the home needs to reach your rent goal, and a property manager reviews the finished work to make sure it's done right.

Do tenants pay for any repairs?

Yes. Every SierraLV lease requires tenants to share in repair costs after their first month in the home. That keeps residents invested in caring for the property and helps your cash flow.

What if the security deposit doesn't cover tenant damage?

In most cases the deposit we collect is enough. In the rare case it isn't, and the former tenant doesn't reimburse you after being billed, we have the legal resources to pursue collection.

Will I receive a 1099?

Owners who earned over $600 in a tax year receive a 1099 before January 31 of the following year — along with a detailed full-year cash-flow report you can share with your tax advisor.
