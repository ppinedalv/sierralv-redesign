Property management services

Nine steps. Every one handled.

Here's exactly what happens from our first conversation to the day one lease ends and the next begins.

  1. 01

    Initial consultation

    A no-obligation meeting to understand your goals. We walk through a rental evaluation based on comparable homes recently leased nearby, a marketing plan, and a realistic timeline to get your home leased.

  2. 02

    Property marketing

    • Complimentary professional photo tour
    • Distribution to MLS and high-traffic rental sites
    • A concise, detailed listing description
    • Showing activity tracking and owner feedback
    • Prompt replies and easy online applications
  3. 03

    Tenant screening

    • FICO credit score
    • National background search
    • Eviction search
    • Employment & income verification
    • Rental history
  4. 04

    Lease negotiation

    A professional, strategic process to get you the most value from your rental — with lease terms written to protect your property.

  5. 05

    Tenant move-in

    • Lease signing with every document completed correctly
    • Documented walk-through with the resident
    • Move-in report with photos — the baseline for move-out
  6. 06

    Tenant support

    • Online rent payments and maintenance requests through the resident portal
    • On-call 24/7 response to emergencies
  7. 07

    Owner reporting & deposits

    • Regular monthly deposit of rental funds
    • Monthly income & balance statements
    • 24/7 owner portal access
    • Year-end 1099 and full-year cash-flow report
  8. 08

    Annual preventative inspections

    A yearly inspection, documented and reported to you, with any items needing attention handled promptly.

  9. 09

    Tenant turnover

    • Final walk-through and move-out report
    • Security deposit calculation and disbursement
    • Project management of make-ready maintenance

How we protect your investment

Policies that work in your favor.

Renter's insurance, always

Required from day one of occupancy. If a resident's coverage lapses, the lease lets us enroll them in a landlord liability policy at their expense — covering accidental tenant damage.

Tenants share repair costs

Every SierraLV lease includes tenant participation in repair costs after the first month — keeping residents invested in the home and protecting your cash flow.

Renewals are earned

Residents must pass an annual inspection and show on-time payments, no HOA violations and reasonable maintenance history before a renewal is offered.

Own an older home? For properties more than 10 years old we recommend a home warranty. Plumbing, electrical, garage doors, water heaters and HVAC systems tend to show wear after that point, and covered repairs save money over time.
