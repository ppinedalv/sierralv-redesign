How to apply.

A simple process with clear expectations — so you know where you stand at every step.

  1. 01

    Find a home & tour it

    Browse current listings and request a showing directly from the listing page.

  2. 02

    Apply online

    Every adult who will live in the home completes an application. We review:

    • FICO credit score
    • National background search
    • Eviction search
    • Employment & income verification
    • Rental history
  3. 03

    Sign your lease

    Once approved, we'll walk you through the lease and make sure every document is completed correctly.

  4. 04

    Get ready to move in

    • Set up utilities in your name before keys are released — see utility setup
    • Provide proof of renter's insurance, effective your first day of occupancy
    • Complete a documented walk-through; we'll record the home's condition with photos
