Referral bonuses

Three simple tiers.

Gold

$560

  • Minimum 1-year management contract
  • Property is vacant
  • 1-year lease term
  • Monthly rent of $2,500 or more

Silver

$400

  • Minimum 1-year management contract
  • Property is vacant
  • 1-year lease term
  • Monthly rent of $1,200–$2,495

Standard

$350

  • Tenant-occupied properties
  • Or monthly rent below $1,200

Referral bonuses are issued per new client referred; multiple properties owned by the same client count as one referral. Referral fees may be subject to withholding or other taxes — referring agents should be aware of the laws in their market regarding referral payments.
