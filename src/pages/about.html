Our story

Personal, face-to-face service.

SierraLV was founded on a simple idea: give real estate investors the knowledge, tools and attention they need to build wealth — and treat every client like a long-term relationship, not a transaction.

As property managers, we keep communication flowing between you and your residents so ownership stays positive and profitable for you, and residents want to stay. Whether you're buying an investment, purchasing a personal home or looking for a partner to preserve and manage a property, we'll always put your best interests first.

Broker / Owner

Allyson Ashihara

Broker Lic# NV B.1003308.LLC · PM# .0166264.BKR · {{EMAIL}}

Born and raised on the Big Island of Hawaii, Allyson moved to Las Vegas nearly 20 years ago and began her career in commercial real estate marketing — which led her to her true calling in property management.

After five years of commercial property management and leasing, she expanded into residential real estate and has since helped many clients build long-term wealth through investment property. Her attention to detail, market knowledge and commitment to service guide and protect every client's investment.

Allyson co-founded SierraLV with Paul Pineda, sharing a vision of a firm centered on integrity, care and genuine relationships. Since Paul's passing in July 2025 she has led the company, continuing that vision with forward-thinking practices that give her clients an advantage in a changing market. Her mission is simple: protect her clients' investments with service rooted in trust, transparency and long-term partnership.

In loving memory

Paul Pineda

With heavy hearts, we remember Paul Pineda, the visionary founder of SierraLV, whose unexpected passing leaves an irreplaceable void in our real estate community. Paul built this company from nothing more than a dream and an unwavering commitment to old-fashioned values, transforming it into the trusted enterprise we know today through sheer determination and his belief that a man's word is his bond.

His mechanical engineering background brought precision to every deal, while his naturally warm personality and famous “aloha smile” made clients feel like family from the very first handshake. Paul wasn't just a serious businessman — he was a devoted husband, loving father of three, and proud companion to his beloved dog Oreo.

Paul's legacy of integrity, warmth and genuine stewardship will continue to guide SierraLV and inspire all who were fortunate enough to know him.
